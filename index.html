The Story Behind The Concept

About Silver Fox Navigator

What Inspired Silver Fox Navigator?

Silver Fox Navigator was created from a lifelong passion for the outdoors. After years of hiking, scrambling through rugged terrain and exploring wild landscapes, the goal became clear: capture the feeling of standing in these incredible places and share that experience with others.

Traditional maps show where a place exists, but they rarely capture the atmosphere, scale and emotion of the landscape. This project combines interactive mapping technology with cinematic drone footage to allow viewers to experience locations from a completely new perspective.

The Places We Visit

The journey explores mountain ranges, dramatic coastlines, remote valleys and hidden trails. A key location is Cadair Idris in Eryri (Snowdonia) National Park, Wales. High-definition drone footage is connected to real-world locations, allowing viewers to explore trails such as the Minffordd Track and discover landmarks including Llyn Cau.

The Gear Behind The Lens

Capturing stable aerial footage in challenging mountain environments requires lightweight and reliable equipment. The DJI Mini series provides the perfect balance between portability, image quality and travel flexibility, allowing cinematic footage to be captured during long hiking adventures.

How To Navigate

Spin, tilt and explore the digital globe using touch controls or a mouse. Select highlighted locations to launch dedicated drone videos, trail guides and landscape films connected to that exact area.
